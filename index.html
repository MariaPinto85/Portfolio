02 / About

A curious mind.

I am Maria Pinto, a data professional with an MSc in Mathematics and a strong interest in business intelligence, statistical modelling and geospatial analysis.

My work focuses on making complex evidence more useful: structuring datasets, checking assumptions, building meaningful indicators and communicating results in a way people can act on.
